## Standing limitations

- **Cancellation policy is uncontrolled.** `rate_basis=any` mixes refundable and non-refundable rates, which differ by 30-40% on an identical room. This is the largest single contamination in the series. The source does not expose the field (a census of 214 live properties found it on none), so it does not clear by changing anything here.
- **Board basis and room type are unknown on every row.** No implemented provider reports them, so room-only and breakfast-inclusive rates are mixed together. A second bias of unknown sign.
- **Regional weights are placeholders** (population-proportional, not expenditure). Every weighted aggregate carries `weights_are_placeholder`.
- **The published series has two methodology breaks** (2025 and February 2026). Comparisons never span them.
